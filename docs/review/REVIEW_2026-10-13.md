## 3. Gender theme precision (three rounds)

Search-only assignment ("Google returned it for the theme query") was unreliable. Judged on
random samples (40 per theme in round 1, 25 in round 2, 20 in round 3):

| Theme | R1 search-only | R2 strict + co-occurrence | R3 after fixes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Violence against women & girls | ~17% | ~72% | ~72% |
| Political & public participation | ~28% | ~68% | ~75–80% |
| Legal status, family & nationality | ~15% | ~64% | ~64% |
| Mobility, dress & "morality" policing | ~55% | ~88% | ~88% |
| Migrant, trafficked & detained women | – | ~40% | ~88% |
| Economic & health rights | – | ~68% | ~68% |
| LGBTQ+ persecution | – | ~43% | 6/8 |

Changes, in order:
1. **Strict gender themes**: a search result counts for a gender theme only if its own title/
   snippet matches the theme's rules; unconfirmed results are kept (inactive) for review/training.
2. **Co-occurrence rules** (per the research guidance: never bare "women"): a women/gender word
   AND an issue word, e.g. "تُغتال النساء", "يقتل ابنتيه", "women + killed".
3. **Arabic normalisation clash fixed**: النائبة ("the female MP") normalised to the same letters
   as ونائبه ("and his deputy"); definite feminine terms no longer generate article-less variants.
4. **Migrant women tightened** to sexual violence / exploitation / pregnancy words (generic
   migrant stories mention "women and children" in passing).
5. **LGBTQ+ requires a Libya mention** even from Libyan outlets (which run world LGBT news).

Remaining known errors: ICC trials naming "rape" among charges (counted under violence: arguably
correct), Egyptian crime stories mentioning a husband "returned from Libya", Libyan outlets'
coverage of women's issues in other countries, municipal-election voter-registration counts.

## 5. Caveats for anyone using the numbers

- **Historical depth is uneven.** Google News returns fewer results for older periods; 2019–2023
  used 30-day windows. Compare the *shape* of a theme over time, not absolute levels across years.
- **Monitored coverage only.** Facebook/Telegram-first reporting is invisible; journalists add it.
- **Recall is limited by design** (strict rules favour precision): domestic killings reported as
  "جريمة قتل" with no gender word are not counted as gender violence.
- **Gender coverage is genuinely small**: in the 4 weeks to 12 Oct 2026, 14 articles on political
  participation, 7 legal, 6 economic/health, 4 violence, 1 mobility, 0 migrant women, 0 LGBTQ+;
  almost none name a district outside Tripoli. That is the information desert the tool exists to show.
