## Reading the numbers

- A second Arabic edition pays off only when it adds Libyan-outlet items the Egypt edition misses (the `new vs EG` column, read with the Libyan share), not when it returns the same headlines again.
- Items from unlabelled outlets are not evidence either way until the register programme labels them (`python -m app.cli label-scopes`).
- Adding an edition doubles the Arabic requests of every six-hourly run and of the Sunday re-query; the collector then stores each story once (ingest.store_article, by URL key).

## What changed in the code

- `gnews.editions` in config/countries/libya.yaml is now a list (one Arabic entry, EG:ar, and one English, GB:en); the theme searches run every entry of a language. Nothing changes until the owner adds an edition here.
- The social-post search reads its editions from `gnews.social_editions` instead of a constant in collectors/gnews_social.py.

## Findings (written after the run)

- Three of the eight requests are not editions at all. `MA:ar`, `TN:ar` and `DZ:ar` were answered with
  a redirect to `EG:ar` (the final URL of every one of those 18 requests carried `ceid=EG:ar`), as was
  `hl=ar&gl=LY`. Google News has no Moroccan, Tunisian, Algerian or Libyan Arabic edition. Their rows
  above are the Egypt edition asked a second time, which is why they match it 100%.
- `SA:ar`, `AE:ar` and `LB:ar` are real editions. On the same theme and week they returned 98 to 99% of
  the same headlines as the Egypt edition: 4 to 5 new headlines each over 6 queries and about 305 items,
  and not one outlet the Egypt edition had not returned. Their Libyan share (42%) is the same as the
  Egypt edition's on the same two weeks.
- So a second Arabic edition would double the Arabic requests of every six-hourly run and of the Sunday
  re-query (about 230 more queries a week) for roughly 1 to 2% more items, none of them from an outlet
  the Egypt edition misses. The edition is not where the coverage is lost: the 100-item cap on economy
  weeks is (the collector already splits capped windows down to a day), and the 25% of items whose
  outlet the register has no scope for (the register programme, `label-scopes`).

## Decision

Recommendation: keep `EG:ar` as the only Arabic edition. Nothing was added to `gnews.editions`. The
owner can add `SA:ar` or `AE:ar` there if a later run (another season, more themes) shows different
numbers (plan Q33).
